Background

About

I am an economist specialising in Public Finance, Urban Economics, and Health Economics. My research uses modern applied econometric methods to evaluate the effects of public policies on crime and health outcomes. A central focus of my work is generating policy-relevant evidence through credible causal inference strategies. I completed all requirements for the Ph.D. in Economics at Northern Illinois University in May 2026, successfully defending my dissertation, Three Essays in Public Finance and Urban Economics.

In addition to my research, I am a dedicated educator with extensive teaching experience across diverse educational settings in both Nigeria and the United States. My experience spans undergraduate instruction in economics as well as nearly a decade of secondary school teaching. These experiences have strengthened my ability to engage students with varying academic backgrounds, learning styles, and career aspirations. I strive to create an inclusive and intellectually stimulating learning environment that promotes critical thinking, analytical reasoning, and lifelong learning.

Through my research and teaching, I seek to contribute to evidence-based policymaking, advance scholarly understanding of pressing socioeconomic issues, and help students develop the skills necessary to succeed in an increasingly data-driven world.

Outside academia, I have been actively involved in Islamic education and community service, including teaching Qur'an and mentoring students in religious studies. These experiences have strengthened my commitment to education, mentorship, and lifelong learning.
